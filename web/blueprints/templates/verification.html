What is the difference between verification modes?

  • None: This verification method is very simple but won't protect you from attackers who can click buttons using their accounts.
  • Math: Shows a math problem in addition of pressing the button to verify.
  • Silly: Makes the user make a bark, meow, or a fox noise to pass the check. This one is just silly and here for no reason.
  • Introduction based: Users write an introduction and admins approve the introduction for the user to get into the server.
